Pipeline: import, edit, export.

Same five stages for manual, AI-assisted, or mixed editing.

  1. STEP_01

    Import

    Add MP4, MOV, MKV or WebM video, plus your own audio. Video joins the timeline automatically.

  2. STEP_02

    Edit

    Trim, split, reorder, remove, and add titles, captions and music.

  3. STEP_03

    Ask

    Describe a change to the AI Director, or skip it and keep editing by hand.

  4. STEP_04

    Review

    Check the proposal against the live timeline, then Apply or Reject. Compare with the original clip any time.

  5. STEP_05

    Export

    Render an H.264 MP4 with AAC audio at the resolution and frame rate you choose. It is validated when it finishes.

Alpha status.

Private evaluation build. Implemented and unimplemented features are listed below.

Implemented

  • Manual editing: trim, split, remove, reorder, titles, captions, volume.
  • Transitions: cross-dissolve, dip to black, fades.
  • Local export: MP4 rendered on your CPU with bundled FFmpeg.
  • AI Director: exact commands, footage-aware requests, approval flow.
  • Safe by design: non-destructive edits, saved before you close, earlier exports kept.

Not implemented

  • Voice commands and still images on the timeline.
  • Relinking media that has been moved or deleted.
  • AI model: not included. Footage analysis needs Ollama and a vision model installed separately, and can take a minute or two.
  • Installer checks: not yet tested on a separate clean PC, and not code-signed.
